# Prompts visuels ChatGPT (22 nov. 2026) — style Roblox 3D des vidéos virales

Règle d'or de ChatGPT : 1) générer une image de RÉFÉRENCE par personnage, 2) une référence par décor, 3) générer chaque scène en image avec ces références, 4) animer en image-vers-vidéo (6–9 s), 5) assembler (voix Étienne, sous-titres, musique). Faire valider la 1re image de chaque personnage avant de lancer les 7 scènes.

## PROMPT MAÎTRE (à mettre au début de chaque prompt de scène)
Create a real animated 3D scene inspired by viral Roblox Storytime cinematics on TikTok. Fully blocky humanoid characters, classic Roblox proportions: square head, rectangular torso, cubic/cylindrical arms and legs, simple hands, minimalist expressive faces (eyes and mouth readable, emotions exaggerated but believable). IMPORTANT: real 3D render, never 2D drawing, never flat illustration, never anime. Clean slightly satin 3D materials with discreet textures. Keep strictly the same clothes, colors, hairstyles, faces and proportions in all scenes. Blocky 3D architecture (houses, doors, windows, furniture), detailed sets with depth and several planes, narrative objects clearly recognizable, consistent environments. Cinematic lighting: global illumination, soft but defined shadows, ambient occlusion, subtle reflections, volumetric lighting, atmospheric depth, lighting adapted to each scene's emotion. Palette: emotional stories = warm beige, brown, soft orange, golden yellow, twilight blue; dark stories = night blue, cold grey, desaturated green, deep black with warm light accents; strong contrast between characters and background. Vertical 9:16 for smartphones, high definition sharp render, wide shots to place locations, medium shots for interactions, close-ups for emotions and reveals, subtle depth of field, camera at character height or slightly low angle, main subject centered with visual space around, subject 40–65% of frame height, central zone kept free for subtitles. Final look: a capture of a high-end 3D Roblox game turned into a cinematic short film. NO TEXT, NO SUBTITLES, NO LOGO, NO WATERMARK.

## Histoire A — Les valises invisibles (palette chaude, lumière dorée)
1. Large establishing shot of a charming blocky suburban town at golden hour. Young male Roblox character (short brown hair, beige sweater, dark blue trousers, white shoes) in foreground looking confused. Colorful 3D suitcases with small glowing golden tags outside different houses. Camera slowly dollies forward toward the character.
2. Same character. Medium-wide shot outside his small house. Seven 3D suitcases suddenly appear in a row on his lawn, each with a golden name tag. He stands on the right, eyes wide, mouth slightly open, hands raised. Golden afternoon light. Camera tracks horizontally across the suitcases, then reveals his shocked face.
3. Close-medium shot. He kneels on the grass and opens an old dark brown suitcase: a handmade child's drawing of two siblings holding hands, folded paper, small red ribbon. Expression goes from curiosity to sadness, drawing held to his chest. Warm sunset rays, dust particles. Camera pushes in on the drawing, then tilts up to his face.
4. Wide shot of the blocky neighborhood. He walks down the sidewalk between houses surrounded by piles of old suitcases; a mother holding an empty suitcase, a child beside a pile, an elderly man looking down sadly. Warm but desaturated sunset, long shadows. Camera follows behind him in a slow tracking shot.
5. Blue hour, modest old house. He stands before the wooden door staring at a tiny faded burgundy suitcase with an old golden tag, kneels, emotional; framed photo of an elderly grandmother visible in the window. Cool blue dusk vs warm amber from inside. Camera moves from medium shot to an intimate close-up of his hand touching the suitcase.
6. Close-up. He sits on the grandmother's front steps holding an old handwritten letter in both hands, face showing grief and regret. Open burgundy suitcase beside him, faded family photo inside. Warm porch light on his face, cool darkening street, blurred background. Camera pushes in on the letter, then focuses on his face.
7. Final scene. He walks alone down the street at sunset carrying the small burgundy suitcase and the letter. Behind him all the other suitcases remain, their golden tags now softly glowing. Warm orange and pink sky. He looks over his shoulder with a bittersweet smile. Camera cranes up and pulls back to reveal the whole quiet neighborhood.

## Histoire B — La fenêtre de minuit (bleu nuit, orange chaud, mélancolique)
1. Wide establishing shot of a quiet old European-style blocky town at midnight. Four-story brick buildings line an empty cobblestone street; every window dark except one tiny warm orange light on the top floor of the building opposite. Narrator (brown hair, navy jacket, black trousers, white shoes) in foreground looking up. Deep blue moonlight, street fog, volumetric light. Camera tilts up from the street to the single lit window.
2. Interior of an old modest fourth-floor apartment. The elderly blocky woman (grey bun, purple cardigan, grey skirt) sits alone by a rectangular window holding a small burning candle close to the glass; gentle, hopeful, slightly melancholic. Wooden table, old chair, framed photograph behind her. Candlelight on her face, deep blue outside. Camera tracks sideways from behind the candle to her face.
3. Same apartment, early evening. The narrator stands near the doorway facing the woman, gesturing toward the candle, confused; she gives a faint peaceful smile. Same furniture, photo and window. Warm amber table lamp, blue evening light. Camera dollies from a two-shot into a closer shot of her smiling face.
4. Emotional close-up. The woman by the window holds a faded old photograph of her young son standing outside the same building. Fragile hopeful expression, lowered eyes. Candle flickers, warm moving shadows, family portrait frame behind. Camera pushes toward the photograph, then to her nostalgic expression.
5. Interior of the narrator's apartment at night. He stands beside his rectangular window, hand on a small table lamp switch. Across the street the old woman's candle still glows. Compassionate expression. Cold blue moonlight, lamp starting to cast warm golden glow on his face. Camera moves from behind his shoulder toward the window, showing both lit apartments.
6. Early morning in the woman's now empty apartment. Wooden chair untouched by the window, candle burned out with a thin trail of smoke, pale morning light. The narrator stands near the table looking down at a small folded envelope beneath the door; sad and surprised. Framed photo remains on the table. Camera tracks from the candle to the envelope, then rises to his face.
7. Final scene. The narrator stands alone in the cobblestone street at sunrise, same navy jacket, holding a small folded letter against his chest, sadness mixed with a gentle smile. The woman's window across the street is dark and empty; golden morning light on the buildings. Camera starts on the letter, then pulls back and rises to reveal the silent street; a single warm light remains in his own window.

## Erreurs à éviter
2D plat / contours dessinés ; personnages qui changent d'une scène à l'autre ; plastique bas de gamme (trop brillant, lumière uniforme) ; décors incohérents ; plans statiques (figurines) ; mauvais cadrage vertical (personnages trop petits, visages coupés).

9:16, 1080×1920, 1 image de référence par scène, vidéo 6–9 s, 30 fps, mouvement faible à modéré, référence de personnage + seed fixe si possible, coupes franches.
