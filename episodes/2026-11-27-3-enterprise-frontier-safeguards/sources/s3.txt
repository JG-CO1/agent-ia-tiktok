  Enterprise Frontier Safeguards is in beta. It’s available under your agreement with Anthropic, such as the Internal use only terms, Commercial Terms of Service and the Service Specific Terms . Settings and behavior can change during the beta.
 With Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS), you bring your own storage and store your own data, under your own access policies and audit logging. Anthropic deletes prompts and responses promptly after the relevant response has been generated and they are written to your bucket. You provide storage for:
 Customer-owned storage keeps a workspace’s data in a bucket in your own cloud account, not in Anthropic-managed storage. Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) requires it for Sealed Safety Data. For Transcripts and Safety Cases, it’s required on Claude Models on Google Cloud (Anthropic managed) . Three categories of data can go into your bucket:
 Customer-owned storage keeps a workspace’s Sealed Safety Data and Transcripts in a bucket in your own cloud account, not in Anthropic-managed storage. Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) requires it for Sealed Safety Data. For Transcripts and Safety Cases, it’s optional on Claude Platform . Three categories of data can go into your bucket:
 Sealed Safety Data : Prompts, responses, and model reasoning, encrypted in Anthropic’s format.
 Transcript: A readable copy of a prompt and response, for your team to read.
 Safety Correspondence: Safety Cases, Safety Notices, and your Safety Case Responses. Never prompts and responses. Your team reviews Safety Cases as Review Safety Cases describes.
 You create the Amazon S3 bucket, an encryption key, and roles in your own cloud account. The key encrypts the bucket, and the roles give Anthropic access to it. You create the Google Cloud Storage bucket, an encryption key, custom roles, and grants to Anthropic’s accounts in your own cloud account. The key encrypts the bucket, and the grants give Anthropic access to it. Your account’s logs record each time Anthropic assumes one of your roles (see Anthropic’s calls in AWS CloudTrail ). You can remove Anthropic’s access. For Sealed Safety Data, requests in the affected workspace may then be refused until access is restored. To remove it, follow the steps in Stop using your bucket in order. To remove it, follow the steps in Stop using your bucket in order. 
 Anthropic doesn’t charge for customer-owned storage, but your cloud provider bills you for storage, reads, writes, and key operations, plus any applicable network transfer.

 Safety Cases, Safety Notices, and your Safety Case Responses (together, Safety Correspondence) never contain your prompts or responses. A Safety Case contains identifiers and details, such as status, timestamps, policy categories, any enforcement action taken, and references to the related Transcripts. Anthropic can read Safety Cases and your Safety Case Responses in your bucket through the review role , but its identities can’t read your Transcripts or write or change your Safety Case Responses. Wherever your Safety Cases are delivered, Anthropic also keeps a record of Safety Correspondence, which authorized Anthropic personnel can read. That record doesn’t contain prompts or responses.
 The two storage settings
 A workspace on the Enterprise Frontier Safeguards card has two storage settings:
 Sealed Safety Data storage sets where Anthropic sends Sealed Safety Data. It’s required, and it offers only your own storage.
 Safety Case and Transcript storage sets where Anthropic sends Transcripts and Safety Cases. It’s optional. Without a bucket under it, your team gets Safety Cases only if your organization has access to the Safety Cases API . Not copied to your storage means that Anthropic doesn’t copy them to your storage.  How long Sealed Safety Data stays
 Sealed Safety Data stays in your bucket for a limited number of days after you send the request. That time is the retention period, and it’s up to 28 days.

 The retention period for Sealed Safety Data is up to 28 days after you send the request.
 Your bucket’s own lifecycle rules delete each object of Sealed Safety Data after it’s 28 days old and permanently remove it at least a day later.
 Anthropic deletes an object from your bucket once everything in it is past the retention period. You don’t set up any cleanup. Old versions and some objects stay longer .
