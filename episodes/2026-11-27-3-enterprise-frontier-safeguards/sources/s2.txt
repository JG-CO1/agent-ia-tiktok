 Enterprise Frontier Safeguards
  Copy page 
  
 Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for eligible organizations that use Claude’s most capable models, with Sealed Safety Data in your own cloud account.
  Copy page 
  
  Enterprise Frontier Safeguards is in beta. It’s available under your agreement with Anthropic, such as the Internal use only terms, Commercial Terms of Service and the Service Specific Terms . Settings and behavior can change during the beta.
 Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) is Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for eligible organizations that use Claude’s most capable models , such as Claude Fable 5.1. Anthropic doesn’t retain your prompts and responses. Sealed Safety Data is written to storage that you own. EFS requires your own bucket. A workspace on Anthropic-managed storage isn’t on EFS, because Anthropic stores encrypted copies of that workspace’s prompts and responses.
 Use EFS when you want Claude’s most capable models but need to keep your Sealed Safety Data in your own cloud account. With EFS, people at your organization, not reviewers at Anthropic, review Safety Cases . EFS includes both:
 Customer-owned storage : A bucket that receives your Sealed Safety Data (prompts, responses, and model reasoning, encrypted in Anthropic’s format). Your team can’t read Sealed Safety Data, even in your own bucket.
 Your team reviews Safety Cases : Anthropic Human Review is always off, so your own team reviews flagged cases that automated safety monitoring finds.

 Anthropic doesn’t charge for EFS, and EFS doesn’t change model behavior, API pricing, or rate limits. Your cloud provider bills you for that storage as usual, including reads, writes, and data egress.
 EFS covers requests to the Messages API in a workspace configured for EFS regardless of model. It does not cover data that is stored by Claude Platform features for their own use, even within a Messages API request, such as code execution containers, Files API uploads, Batch API results, and Claude Managed Agents sessions. See Data and retention in the FAQ.
 Get access
 To get EFS, follow these steps:
 If the Programs page in the Claude Console already shows Enterprise Frontier Safeguards , skip to step 2. Otherwise, contact your Anthropic account team to request access. If you don’t have an account team, complete the Enterprise Frontier Safeguards request form .
 Once Anthropic confirms that your organization is eligible, an Organization Admin accepts the terms from the Programs page in the Claude Console.
 To set up EFS, follow the EFS setup guide for your platform .
 On Amazon Bedrock, you get access through AWS. See Enterprise Frontier Safeguards on Amazon Bedrock .
 On Claude Platform, EFS applies to a whole workspace
 Once EFS takes effect for a workspace, it applies to every request to every model in that workspace, including models that don’t require data retention.
 To use EFS for only some traffic, use separate workspaces. The API key or workspace ID that you send determines which card a request uses. A new workspace leaves your existing workspaces and their API keys as they are.
 Enterprise Data Controls
 On Claude Platform, accepting the EFS and Enterprise Data Controls terms gives you both. With Enterprise Data Controls, you can use a customer-managed key or turn off Anthropic Human Review on Anthropic-managed storage. With either one, Access Transparency is on for your organization.
